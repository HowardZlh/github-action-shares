<a href="https://failrouter.com"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FailRouter/failrouter/main/public/og.png" alt="Failrouter, Museum of Failed Routes: outage exhibits drawn as routes, one small change followed by a few hops to the failure"></a>

Each exhibit is one outage drawn as the route it took: a small change, a few hops, then a very bad day. All 12 are condensed from a public postmortem or investigation report, and each card names its source.

## Start with these three

| Exhibit | The route in one line |
|:--|:--|
| [CrowdStrike outage, July 2024](https://failrouter.com/exhibits/crowdstrike-2024/) | One content update, about 8.5 million Windows machines boot-looping on a blue screen |
| [Facebook outage, October 2021](https://failrouter.com/exhibits/facebook-2021/) | A maintenance command cut every backbone link; DNS withdrew its routes for about 6 hours |
| [AWS S3 outage, February 2017](https://failrouter.com/exhibits/s3-2017/) | One mistyped playbook input, S3 in us-east-1 down for about 4 hours |

Then walk the [3D hall](https://failrouter.com/hall/), where every exhibit hangs in a gallery next to a sculpture of its impact score, or read [how impact is scored](https://failrouter.com/impact/) on five dimensions.

## Add an outage

Know a public postmortem that belongs here? [Open an issue](https://github.com/FailRouter/failrouter/issues/new) with the link, or follow [Add an exhibit](https://github.com/FailRouter/failrouter#add-an-exhibit) and send a PR. Two rules: only facts from the report, and describe systems and decisions, not people.

If an exhibit ever helped you explain a risk to your team, a ⭐ on [FailRouter/failrouter](https://github.com/FailRouter/failrouter) helps the next SRE find the museum.
